## 1. Education

I contributed to educational outreach activities
aimed at making synthetic biology more accessible
to students and the general public.

My activities included planning and supporting
educational events and developing ways to communicate
synthetic biology to audiences with different levels
of scientific knowledge.

### Lab Workshop

I helped organize and support a hands-on synthetic biology
laboratory workshop for middle and high school students.

The workshop introduced participants to synthetic biology
and basic laboratory equipment through lectures,
experiments, and Q&A sessions.
