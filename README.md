# iGEM HG-Tokyo 2025

## Overview

In 2025, I participated in a competition called iGEM by being part of HG-Tokyo,
a high school team competing in the iGEM High School Section.

Our project focused on developing new approaches to hay fever
using narirutin biosynthesis and a fusion peptide.

### Team

- Team: HG-Tokyo
- Competition: iGEM 2025 — High School Section

### Team Achievement

- Gold Medal
- Best Presentation — High School, Nominee
- Best Integrated Human Practices — High School, Nominee
- Best Village Project — High School, Nominee

## Project Overview

Our project addressed hay fever through two complementary approaches:

1. Narirutin Biosynthesis
2. Fusion Peptide

For the narirutin approach, we aimed to biosynthesize
narirutin, a flavonoid mainly found in Jabara,
using yeast.

For the fusion peptide approach, we designed a fusion peptide
using immunomodulatory peptides and cleavable linkers.

Our overall goal was to develop a comprehensive approach
to hay fever and ultimately incorporate these approaches
into a nasal spray.
