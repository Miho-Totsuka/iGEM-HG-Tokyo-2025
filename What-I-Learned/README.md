# What I Learned

### Scientific Communication
I learned how to explain synthetic biology
to people with different levels of scientific knowledge.

### International Collaboration
I experienced planning and communicating with
students from another country through the collaboration
with Imperial College London.

### Research
I learned that research ideas evolve through discussion,
feedback, and interaction with researchers.

### Project Management
I learned that a scientific project requires more than
experiments. Education, finance, communication,
and coordination are also important.

## Science and Society
Through Education and Human Practices activities,
I learned to consider how scientific research
can interact with society and different communities.
